THE EDSON HOUSE

Make yourself at home.

Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan. Choose a floor to see inside, click a floor for room details, or click a door to open it.

In walkthrough, use WASD and the mouse. E operates the door you are looking at. Escape releases the mouse; Exit Walkthrough returns to presentation.

Traced from the published Edson House floor plans. Printed room dimensions are retained; shell scale, heights, roof pitches, stairs and finishes are approximate. The stair flight is an inferred reconstruction within the shown void, not a surveyed stair design.

Measure: click two surfaces for a distance; Clear removes measurements. m / ft switches units. Dimensions and measurements are indicative.
